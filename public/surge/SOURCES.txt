Coastal still-water levels by return period — source and terms
===============================================================

ewl-stations.json  125 NOAA CO-OPS water-level stations with an Extreme Water Levels analysis: station id, name, state,
                   location, tidal datum epoch, MHHW above NAVD88 (null where the station has no NAVD88 tie) and above
                   MSL, the annual exceedance probability levels 99 %, 50 %, 10 %, 2 % and 1 % above MHHW (high water,
                   GEV fit to annual maxima), the 90 % confidence interval of the 1 % level, and the NOAA 2022
                   relative sea-level-rise medians (Low ... High) at 2050 / 2080 / 2100. JSON (format: src/surge/stations.ts),
                   38.5 kB.

SOURCES         NOAA National Ocean Service, Center for Operational Oceanographic Products and Services (CO-OPS):
                  Extreme Water Levels   https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/dpapi/prod/webapi/product.json?name=extremewaterlevels
                                         (&station=<id>&details=exceedancelevels&levelType=high&units=metric)
                                         https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/est/
                  Tidal datums           https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/mdapi/prod/webapi/stations/<id>/datums.json?units=metric
                  Sea-level rise         https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/dpapi/prod/webapi/product/slr_projections.json
                                         ?station=<id>&units=metric&report_year=2022&affil=US
                                         (Sweet et al. 2022, Global and Regional Sea Level Rise Scenarios for the United
                                         States, NOAA Technical Report NOS 01; medians, zeroed at 2005)
                Downloaded October 2026. 126 stations are listed; Port Mansfield (8778490) returned no exceedance levels.

TERMS           Public domain (U.S. Government work). Acknowledge NOAA/NOS/CO-OPS.

CHECK           Fort Myers (8725520) reproduces the Fort Myers preset's hand-transcribed ladder (src/loss/ladders.ts,
                ewl-8725520) to the centimetre on NAVD88, and its sea-level-rise record equals the climate layer's

HOW IT WAS MADE scripts/bake-surge.ts:   node --import tsx scripts/bake-surge.ts

WHY BAKED       The CO-OPS APIs do send Access-Control-Allow-Origin: * (checked October 2026), so a page could call them;
                the table is small, Site check must work offline like every other ladder, and baking adds no endpoint to
                the content-security policy.

WHAT IT IS NOT  Not NOAA SLOSH: the SLOSH Maximum of Maximums are per hurricane category (no annual chance) and the public
                National Storm Surge Hazard Maps are binned depths above ground, not water levels. The extreme water
                levels are a still-water statistic at the gauge: tide + surge + the wave setup the gauge feels, no wave
                run-up, stationary (fitted as if sea level did not rise).
