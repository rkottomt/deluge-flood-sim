NOAA Atlas 14 rain return periods — source and terms
====================================================

pf60m-pds.bin   1-hour (60-minute) precipitation-frequency estimates at 2, 10, 25, 100 and 500 years, partial-duration
                series, in mm/h, for the United States: CONUS (1/12 degree), Alaska (1/6 degree), Hawaii and Puerto Rico /
                US Virgin Islands (1/24 degree). Gzip-compressed binary (format: src/data/atlas14.ts), 0.73 MB.

SOURCE          NOAA Atlas 14, Precipitation-Frequency Atlas of the United States, Volumes 1 (Semiarid Southwest),
                2 (Ohio River Basin and Surrounding States), 3 (Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands), 4 (Hawaiian
                Islands), 6 (California), 7 (Alaska), 8 (Midwestern States), 9 (Southeastern States), 10 (Northeastern
                States), 11 (Texas) and 12 (Interior Northwest). NOAA / National Weather Service, Office of Water
                Prediction, Hydrometeorological Design Studies Center (HDSC).
                Grids: https://hdsc.nws.noaa.gov/pub/hdsc/data/<region>/<region><T>yr60ma.zip
                (ESRI ASCII, 30 arc-seconds, NAD83, 60-minute depth in 1/1000 inch, -9 = no data), downloaded October 2026.
                Point service used to check the result: https://hdsc.nws.noaa.gov/pfds/ (PFDS).

TERMS           Public domain. Each grid's FGDC metadata: "n/a, no restrictions apply and data is public domain."
                Use constraint: "Acknowledgement of NOAA/National Weather Service, Office of Water Prediction,
                Hydrometeorological Design Studies Center (HDSC)" — given in the app's site reports (src/loss/ladders.ts
                LADDER_SOURCES['noaa-atlas14']), the README and here. "Users must assume responsibility to determine the
                appropriate use of these data."

HOW IT WAS MADE scripts/bake-atlas14.ts. Each output cell is the mean of the valid 30-arc-second source cells inside it,
                taken from one volume (where volumes overlap at their borders, the one covering more of the cell; ties go
                to the newer volume). Intensity = 60-minute depth (in) x 25.4 mm. Stored in 0.1 mm/h steps.
                Reproduce:  node --import tsx scripts/bake-atlas14.ts --download --cache=<dir>
                            node --import tsx scripts/bake-atlas14.ts --cache=<dir>
                Check:      node --import tsx scripts/bake-atlas14.ts --verify=tests/fixtures/atlas14
                At the eleven captured PFDS points (Houston, Asheville, Denver, Phoenix, Los Angeles, Missoula, Miami,
                Boston, Honolulu, San Juan, Anchorage) the grid is within 3.4 % of the PFDS 1-hour value at every return
                period (PFDS prints whole mm/h, so part of that is its rounding).

NOT COVERED     Western Washington and Oregon (Atlas 14 Volume 12 covers only Idaho, Montana and Wyoming of the
                Northwest; the coast is still on NOAA Atlas 2), the western Aleutians beyond 180 degrees, Guam and the
                Pacific islands (Volume 5). The app reports "no NOAA Atlas 14 estimate" there and offers no rain ladder.

WHY NOT LIVE    The PFDS sends no CORS headers (checked October 2026), so a browser page cannot read it. NOAA Atlas 15 will
                supersede Atlas 14; as of October 2026 only a Montana pilot is published, with CONUS preliminary estimates
                "no earlier than September 2026" and published estimates "no earlier than September 2027"
                (https://water.noaa.gov/about/atlas15). Re-bake from Atlas 15 grids once they are published.
